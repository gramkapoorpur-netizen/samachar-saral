ससमाचार सरलPrivacy

Privacy Policy

Bookmarks and reading history in this MVP are saved only in your browser local storage. Production login, analytics and ads should use a full privacy notice before launch.

Advertising

The layout contains AdSense-ready placeholders. Actual ads should be added only after Google approval and policy review.

Audio

Browser text-to-speech reads page text aloud. If cloud TTS is enabled later, generated audio files should be cached without storing unnecessary personal data.
